Where these viewer counts come from

Data: SullyGnome.com (https://sullygnome.com), hourly Twitch category averages.

The practice cases (p00..p03) and every case's comparables are past game launches' Twitch categories as SullyGnome
publishes them: per hour, the category's average concurrent viewers and its average number of live channels. A point
at `at` covers the hour [at, at + 1 h) in UTC; an hour SullyGnome did not sample has no point (a small category is
sampled only about every 12 hours, so its quiet days are sparse). Nothing is interpolated. The points were read once,
on 2026-11-17, from the JSON behind each game's page (one calendar month a request):

  GET https://sullygnome.com/api/charts/linecharts/getconfig/GameViewers/30/0/<sullygnome id>/<name>/%20/%20/<YYYY>/<M>/%20/0/
  GET https://sullygnome.com/api/charts/linecharts/getconfig/GameChannels/30/0/<sullygnome id>/<name>/%20/%20/<YYYY>/<M>/%20/0/

(the "Average viewers" and "Channels" datasets), for the release date minus 14 days to the release date plus 3 days;
for Grand Theft Auto VI's own category, every point from its first (2026-08-27T17:00Z) to 2026-11-17T02:00Z. Each
Twitch category id was checked against Twitch's own directory (https://gql.twitch.tv/gql, game(id) and game(name))
on 2026-11-17.

Terms: SullyGnome publishes no open licence; its footer reads "SullyGnome.com © is in no way affiliated with Twitch.
Please do not scrape this site, it's a hobby project with limited resources." The figures are factual counts of
Twitch's public directory, quoted here with attribution to SullyGnome. Please do not bulk-download the site to extend
them; the pages below show each curve.

A practice case is a launch's UTC release day: its day_start is 00:00 UTC of the release date, its checkpoint one hour
later, its known hour 1 the mean of the launch's points in the first hour, and its history the launch's points from 14
days before the release date up to the checkpoint. Its notes give the launch's real unlock, which is rarely midnight
UTC. An hour's figure here is SullyGnome's hourly average (one point an hour), not the mean of 60 minute reads.

The final case (h00) is Grand Theft Auto VI's release day, 2026-11-19 UTC. Its history, its known hour 1 and the
hours it is scored on are make.fun's own recorder's minute reads (collector/twitch-ccu in the make.fun repository) of
Twitch's public directory count for the category (Twitch game id 1867279146), once a minute:

  POST https://gql.twitch.tv/gql   (header Client-Id: kimne78kx3ncx6brgo4mv6wki5h1ko, Twitch's public web client id)
  { game(id: "1867279146") { id name viewersCount broadcastersCount } }

viewersCount is the concurrent live viewers Twitch's directory page shows for the category, broadcastersCount its live
channels. An hour's figure in the final is the mean of that hour's valid minute reads, rounded half up, when the hour
has at least 30 of them (hour 1, at least one).
